Cách tính ROI Score
ROI = (Gain_score × 2) / Complexity_score

Gain score: Low=1 · Medium=2 · High=3 · Very High=4
Complexity score: Trivial=0.5 · Easy=1 · Medium=2 · Hard=3 · Very Hard=5

ROI > 6 = Quick Win (implement ngay) · 3–6 = Medium · < 3 = Research only
